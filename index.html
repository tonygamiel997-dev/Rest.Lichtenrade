About the house

Family-run since day one

Sole is the kind of place guests come back to on purpose — regulars know the servers by name, and the servers know exactly how you like your carbonara. Guests consistently mention the warmth of the team as much as the food itself, with several reviewers singling out how attentive and genuinely friendly the staff are, visit after visit.

The dining room fills up around a bright sunroom that guests often ask for by name, and the kitchen keeps a rotating list of daily specials alongside the classics — the Pizza Napoli and Pizza Crudaiola are the two dishes people order again and again.
